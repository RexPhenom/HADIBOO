Her story

People thrive when they have the right knowledge, guidance and support.

HADIBOO is the professional brand of Hadassah-Esther Nsibietabasi, a multidisciplinary professional passionate about helping individuals discover their potential, build meaningful careers, access opportunities, and contribute to sustainable development.

With over seven years of experience spanning public health, community development, programme implementation, capacity building, career development, and professional mentorship, Hadassah-Esther has built her work around a central belief: people thrive when they have access to the right knowledge, guidance, opportunities, and support systems.

Her professional journey reflects a commitment to improving lives through practical interventions, empowering individuals, strengthening communities, and supporting organisations to achieve meaningful results.

Professional background & expertise

As an award-winning public health and development professional, Hadassah-Esther has contributed to initiatives focused on community health, environmental health, cancer awareness and advocacy, health education, youth development, gender-based violence prevention, and community empowerment.

Her experience includes programme planning and implementation, stakeholder engagement, project coordination, community mobilisation, research, monitoring and evaluation, training facilitation, and capacity strengthening.

Through her work with development organisations and community-based initiatives, she has supported health awareness campaigns, community outreach programmes, stakeholder collaborations, and interventions designed to improve access to health information and essential services.

Career development

Closing the gap between qualification and opportunity.

Beyond her work in public health and development, Hadassah-Esther is deeply committed to helping students, graduates, and young professionals navigate the complexities of career development.

Recognising the gap between academic qualifications and professional opportunities, she established HADIBOO Career Clinic, a career development and professional positioning platform dedicated to helping individuals gain clarity, identify their strengths, communicate their value, and prepare for opportunities.

Through career guidance, CV development, LinkedIn optimisation, interview preparation, mentorship, and practical training, she supports individuals in building the confidence and professional readiness needed to compete in today's evolving job market.

Her approach goes beyond helping people secure employment. She encourages individuals to understand their abilities, develop relevant skills, demonstrate their contributions, and make informed decisions about their professional growth.

She has also contributed to career development and mentorship communities, including Prof Dee Career Clinic, supporting learning, professional visibility, and opportunity readiness.
