FAQ

Good questions.

Who are the career services for?

Students, graduates, young professionals and career-changers who want clarity, stronger documents and interview confidence.

What is an ATS-friendly CV?

Many employers use Applicant Tracking Systems to screen CVs. An ATS-friendly CV is structured and worded so these systems — and people — can read it easily.

Do you work with organizations?

Yes — NGOs, community organizations, schools, companies and development teams, for training, programme support, proposals and capacity assessments.

Can sessions be held online?

Yes. Most sessions and trainings can be delivered online; in-person arrangements are available on request.

Why do job listings disappear after six days?

To keep the board current. Always apply through the employer's official link and never pay to apply.
